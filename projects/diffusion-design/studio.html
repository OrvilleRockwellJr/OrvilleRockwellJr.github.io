      Design gene-editing guide sequences

      The same engine, a different alphabet. It generates 30-nucleotide SpCas9 guide contexts (4 nt, a 20-nt protospacer, the NGG PAM, 3 nt). A classifier ensemble trained on noised sequences steers toward high cutting efficiency; a held-out logistic model trained on a different lab’s library scores the result.
