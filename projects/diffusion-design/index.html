Generative design that audits its own answers.

Steer small molecules and CRISPR guides toward PTPσ, the receptor that lets the glial scar block axon regrowth after stroke. Every candidate is audited by a second model that never saw the steering, so a score the generator learned to game gets flagged instead of shipped.

At a glance

Question

When can a steering predictor be trusted during generative design, and when does gradient guidance beat generate-and-filter?

Approach

Classifier-guided diffusion for molecules and CRISPR guides, a 5-head bootstrap critic ensemble with an uncertainty gate, and a held-out judge trained on different features and different data.

Finding

For molecules the gate decides whether the critic’s shortlist is usable (5% pass the audit without it, 72% with it; unguided filtering 31%). For guides filtering already reaches 92% and the gate did not help.

Limits

Small public datasets, one target (PTPRS), and a judge that is a model, not an experiment. Nothing here is lab-validated. Method and reproduction.
