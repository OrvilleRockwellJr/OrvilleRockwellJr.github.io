Classifier-Guided Diffusion for Post-Stroke Drug Design

A working generative platform for one target, PTPσ, the receptor through which the glial scar blocks axon regrowth after stroke. Describe what you want and what it is for, and one guided-diffusion engine designs both kinds of candidate: small molecules and gene-editing guide sequences, aimed at the same target. It runs entirely in your browser: no server, no API, nothing uploaded.

Research prototype, in silico only. Every score is a prediction from small models trained on public data (ChEMBL, B3DB, Tox21, the Doench guide-efficacy screens). Nothing here is lab-validated, and nothing here is medical advice. The Evidence tab shows how often the held-out judge agrees with the steering classifiers, including where it does not.

How I read it

    What lane this is, and the open gap

    Not Evo 2. Evo 2 is a billion-parameter genome model. This studio uses small models trained on public data, so it is a testbed for one question, not a competitor.

    The lane. Evo 2 is steered by generating many sequences and keeping the ones an outside predictor scores well (search and filter). Here the predictor's gradient steers every denoising step, and a second model that never saw the steering checks the result. The report on each guide shows where those two models agree.

    The gap. When can a steering predictor be trusted, and when does gradient guidance beat generating more and filtering? Our evidence is mixed. For molecules, guidance without the uncertainty gate made the critic-ranked shortlist worse than no guidance (9% vs 49% pass the held-out judge, so the critic was being exploited); with the gate, 81% pass (guidance 4). For guides, filtering unguided samples already reaches 90%, guidance lifts that to about 100%, and the gate did not help. The Evidence tab has the tables and error bars.

    Design small molecules

    A diffusion model denoises a molecular latent code into a structure. Three classifiers, trained on noised codes, steer every denoising step toward brain penetration, PTP-family activity and low toxicity. A separate held-out judge, built on different features, scores the result.

    0 = unguided diffusion. Higher pushes harder on the classifiers.

    Design gene-editing guide sequences

    The same engine, a different alphabet. It generates 30-nucleotide SpCas9 guide contexts (4 nt, a 20-nt protospacer, the NGG PAM, 3 nt). A classifier ensemble trained on noised sequences steers toward high cutting efficiency; a held-out logistic model scores the result. This is the proof of mechanism for sequence design; to target PTPRS itself, use the scanner.
