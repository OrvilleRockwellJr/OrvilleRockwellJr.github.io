Questions I want to pursue

What I would like to work on in a PhD, each one grown out of my research.

  1. How can a learned system tell when its own estimates, whether a camera pose, a depth map or a predicted binding score, are not yet good enough to act on?

    From multi-camera calibration at Brown to score calibration in closed-loop discovery, small, invisible errors tend to become confident downstream choices.

  2. What should a robot sense, and what should it imagine, to keep working when its sensors fail or the world looks unfamiliar?

    The GPS-denied drone and the underwater ROV lose their view in different ways: blur at speed in open air, murk with little to track. Fusion, learned world models and generative simulation may let one source cover for another.

  3. Can the tools of vision and generative modeling help design molecules and genomes, and close the loop by deciding which designs deserve an experiment?

    Cryo-EM is 3D reconstruction, and AlphaFold 3 and RFdiffusion are diffusion over coordinates instead of pixels. My closed-loop work suggests that deciding which outputs to trust deserves as much attention as generating them.
