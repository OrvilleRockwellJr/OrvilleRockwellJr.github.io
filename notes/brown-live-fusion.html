Two Raspberry Pis, one synchronized view

Screen recording, 25 seconds, no sound. The live fusion view of the multi-camera rig for GPS-denied drone navigation (Prof. Benjamin Kimia's group, LEMS, Brown University). Two Raspberry Pi 5s, each on its own Ethernet link, stream synchronized capture into one page; the status line for each camera shows its frame rate, frame count and the synchronization offset between the two. The browser shows each stream with its own network delay, so on-screen frames are not frame-locked; timing comes from the capture logs.
